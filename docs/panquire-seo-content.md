## How to choose an adult electric dirt bike

Choose an adult electric dirt bike based on where you ride, how the bike fits, and how much battery capacity you need for your usual outings. Then compare the vehicle price and the total cost for your destination.

### Start with your riding surface and fit

The Panquire T-01 and T-02 are recreational off-road models with 19-inch front and 17-inch rear wheels. Confirm that your intended riding area permits the exact vehicle. Use each model’s geometry as a starting point, then consider your inseam, whether you can balance the bike comfortably at a stop, and how the suspension feels under your weight. The listed 150 kg maximum load and the roughly 40–95 kg suspension rider-weight guidance measure different things. Ask Panquire about fit and setup if you’re unsure.

### Compare battery and charging together

The [T-01](/products/t-01) has a removable 60V 31.2Ah battery with 1.872 kWh nominal energy. The [T-02](/products/t-02) has a removable 72V 41Ah battery with 2.952 kWh nominal energy. Both use electronic power levels selected from the handlebar. Choose the T-02 if more peak power and a larger battery matter most. Consider the T-01 if lower vehicle weight and a lower vehicle price are your priorities.

Panquire states an Eco-mode range of 80 km for the T-01 and 90 km for the T-02. Actual range varies with speed, terrain, load, temperature and riding conditions. Use the model-specific charger and check its charging time against the time you have between rides.

### Check the total before ordering

Check current vehicle prices in the [adult electric dirt bike collection](/collections/electric-dirt-bikes). Free outbound shipping is included for supported destinations. Applicable taxes or duties, riding gear and upkeep can affect your budget. Check the [shipping](/policies/shipping-policy) and [return](/policies/refund-policy) terms, and read the [limited warranty](/pages/tos#warranty) covering the motor, battery and controller.

### Your buying checklist

- Confirm the riding surface and permitted use.
- Check bike dimensions, suspension guidance and fit.
- Compare the stated range and model-specific charging time.
- Confirm the current vehicle price and destination-specific total.
- Ask [Panquire support](/pages/contact) about parts and warranty service before ordering if either affects your choice.
